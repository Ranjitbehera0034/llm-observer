# LLM Observer launch kit

Status: draft marketing assets. Publish after live purchase and installation checks; no campaign has been sent or funded.

## Offer

Know where your AI coding budget goes. LLM Observer brings supported coding-tool usage into one local dashboard. Explore expensive sessions, compare projects, and identify potential waste. Start free with no account required for local tracking.

Pro: $9/month or $79/year, 100 projects and 90-day request retention. India: ₹799 for a 30-day pass, no auto-renewal. Teams: pilot enquiries only. Payment-provider products must match these prices before enabling checkout.

## 45-second demo script

0–8 seconds: “Using several AI coding tools? See their supported session usage in one place.” Show a real, permission-cleared Sessions view.
8–20: Sort expensive sessions and open one. Explain estimated API-equivalent usage versus money actually billed.
20–32: Compare projects and review one optimization suggestion. Explain its assumptions; do not claim guaranteed savings.
32–40: Show local setup and Free/Pro limits.
40–45: “Try LLM Observer free. Upgrade when you need more projects and longer request history.”

20 activated users, five paying customers, and three permission-based examples of a useful decision. Track installation completion, time to first result, weekly return, payment-to-activation success, refunds, support burden and repeat use. Review after one month before increasing ad spend.
